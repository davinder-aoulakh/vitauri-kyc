Usage: --bg page background; --surface cards and bubbles' base; --surface2 bank bubble and subtle fills; --ink text; --muted secondary text; --line borders; --brand primary action, client bubble, active section; --ok / --warn / --bad / --ai for pills and checks (each with a -soft background).

2. TYPE
System font stack: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'SF Pro Text', 'Segoe UI', Roboto, 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif. Mono for small labels and pills: ui-monospace, 'SF Mono', Menlo, Consolas. Base size 15 px, line-height 1.5. No web fonts (the prototype loads nothing external).

3. LAYOUT
- Max width 1120 px, centred. Two columns on wide screens: left rail (about 300 px) and the conversation card.
- Cards: radius 12-16 px, 1 px --line border, soft shadow.
- Bubbles: bank bubble left with round avatar "N" (analyst messages use initials, for example "SV"), client bubble right in --me color. Max width of a bubble about 70% of the thread.
- Widgets sit left-aligned under the question, same max width as bubbles (review and tracker may be wider, up to 620 px).
- Pills: mono 10.5 px, rounded, with soft background in ok/warn/bad/ai/brand.

4. RESPONSIVE (phone, below about 860 px wide)
- One column. The left rail collapses: hide greeting text, deadline card, help line, open-requests box, section list and the saved indicator; keep the progress bar and percentage sticky at the top.
- The message composer (after submission) is sticky at the bottom.
- Demo controls are collapsed (prototype only).
- ID&V: below 760 px (the smaller of window width and screen width) show the button instead of the QR code.
- Keep a 16 px side gutter and honour safe-area insets (iPhone notch and home bar). Set viewport-fit=cover.

5. INPUTS
- Font size 16 px minimum in inputs (prevents zoom on iOS Safari).
- Touch targets at least 40 px high. Chips wrap to several lines.
- Keyboard: Enter sends a single-line text answer; labels via aria-label when there is no visible label.
- Focus ring: 2 px --brand, 2 px offset.

6. ACCESSIBILITY
- The thread is an aria-live="polite" region. New bank messages are announced.
- Structure card: SVG with role="img" and aria-label; the text list that follows is the accessible alternative.
- Do not rely on color alone: pills carry text; checks carry a symbol (check mark or exclamation mark).
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion (spinner and typing dots stop animating).
- Contrast: target at least 4.5:1 for body text in both themes. The prototype's contrast was not measured with a tool: run a contrast check on the final colors, and on any tenant brand color.

7. MOTION
Typing indicator before every bank message (3 dots). Auto-scroll the last element into view after each message (smooth). Document checks reveal one by one (about 0.5 s apart). Keep total delays short: the delays exist to feel like a conversation, not to slow the client down. Make them skippable when prefers-reduced-motion is set.

8. PHONE TESTING NOTE
Test on a real iPhone (Safari) and a real Android phone: the signature canvas (touch), the QR hand-off to the phone, safe areas, and scroll behaviour in the thread. The prototype was tested in a desktop browser with phone emulation only.
